NTN (Japan)

Authentic
  • Proprietary serialized barcode on green NTN packaging.
  • Micro-honed bearing raceways with near-zero friction.
  • Premium cast iron housing markings on all UCP pillow units.

The True Danger of Duplicate Bearings in Nepal

Cheap duplicate bearings flood the market at 30-40% cheaper prices. In rice mills, crushers, and cement plants, they invariably lead to catastrophic machine failure:

  • Sudden Bearing Seizure: Inferior heat treatment causes balls to shatter under load, locking the shaft instantly.
  • Electric Motor Burnout: Excess friction causes motor overheating and coil burnout, costing lakhs in rewinding.
  • Costly Plant Downtime: A single Rs. 800 fake bearing can stop an entire rice processing line during peak harvest season.
