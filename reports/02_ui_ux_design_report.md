## 2. Mobile Ergonomics for Field Mechanics & Buyers
Over 75% of industrial spare parts queries in Nepal originate on smartphones from mechanics or plant supervisors standing right next to stopped machinery.

1. **Touch Target Sizing**:
   - All critical CTA buttons (`Quick WhatsApp RFQ`, `Call Sales`, `Call Warehouse`) maintain a minimum touch target of `48px × 48px`.
   - Generous finger-friendly padding prevents mis-clicks in noisy, high-vibration factory environments.

2. **Persistent Floating Action Bar**:
   - Floating WhatsApp button (`FAB`) is pinned to the bottom-right corner with a pulsing emerald ripple effect.
   - Does not obscure product catalog cards or footer navigation links.

3. **High-Contrast Outdoor Readability**:
   - Sunlight-friendly typography with contrast ratio exceeding `7.5:1` on all text elements (well above WCAG AA 4.5:1 requirement).

## 3. 4-Column Wholesale Catalog Hierarchy
The catalog is organized into 4 distinct functional quadrants:
1. **Precision Bearings**: Deep groove ball bearings, spherical roller bearings, taper roller bearings, and pillow blocks (SKF, NBC, URB, NTN).
2. **Conveyor Belts & Industrial Pulleys**: Heavy nylon-fabric conveyor belting, Chevron belts, timing belts, and cast-iron V-pulleys.
3. **Machinery Spare Parts**: Industrial oil seals, chain sprockets, couplings, and mechanical hardware.
4. **Machine Workshop Services**: Precision lathe machining, bearing press fitting, shaft rebuilding, and dynamic balancing.
