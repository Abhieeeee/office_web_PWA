# Shree Anjani Belt & Bearing — Internal Operations System (Phase 2)

This directory is separated from the public customer-facing B2B website and will house the store's internal tools:

## Planned Modules
1. **Warehouse Inventory & Rack Locater**: Bin tracking for SKF, NBC, URB, NTN, and V-belts.
2. **Dealer Proforma & VAT Invoicing**: Rapid generation of Nepali tax invoices & delivery receipts.
3. **Regional Freight & Transport Logger**: Dispatch tracking to Pokhara, Kathmandu, Butwal, Birgunj, Nepalgunj.
4. **Workshop Job Card Manager**: Machine lathe, keyway cutting, and pulley rebore orders.
